## 🧩 About

**Raft Trainer** is a curated list of Raft open-source tools:

- **Mod Menus** — item spawner, god mode, speedhack, teleport, no build cost
- **UI Automation Bots** — auto-farm, auto-collect, resource ESP for single-player study
- **Quality-of-Life Mods** — unlimited durability, easy crafting, overlay helpers
- **Research Frameworks** — training configs and notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools like **RaftModLoader**, **RaftModding**, and **UnityExplorer**.
